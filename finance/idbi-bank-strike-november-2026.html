IDBI Bank Strike on Nov 2–3, 2026: Customer Services & Borrower Impact

IDBI Bank employees began a two-day nationwide strike on 2 November. Here is what account holders should know on 3 November about branch work, digital banking and temporary ATM fee relief.

LoansBharti Editorial Desk · 3 November 2026, 6:00 AM IST · 5 min read · Updated 3 November 2026

Image credit: Arunvrparavur / Wikimedia Commons, licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0. File photograph from 24 January 2015; not a current strike image.

Agar aapka IDBI Bank mein account hai, to 3 November is two-day employee strike ka second scheduled day hai. Bloomberg ki 2 November report, Moneycontrol par published, ke mutabik employees ne nationwide strike shuru kar di thi. Bank ne customers ko digital channels use karne ko kaha hai aur dono strike dates ke liye other-bank ATMs par cash withdrawal charges se temporary relief announce kiya hai. Simple words mein, iska matlab yeh nahi ki IDBI Bank ki har service band hai.

Quick update: IDBI Bank’s advisory says Go Mobile+, Net Banking, UPI, ATMs and Customer Service Points (CSPs) will remain operational on 2–3 November. It also says cash withdrawals from non-IDBI ATMs will be free of charge during these two days. Branch counter services may still face delays depending on staff participation.

Kya hua?

In a filing dated 31 October 2026, IDBI Bank informed the stock exchanges that it had received a strike notice for 2 and 3 November from the All India IDBI Officers’ Association (AIIDBIOA) and the All India Industrial Development Bank Employees’ Association (AIIDBEA). The filing says the notice was in support of the associations’ demands; it does not describe those demands in detail.

On 2 November, Bloomberg reported that IDBI Bank employees had started a nationwide strike. The report, citing a statement by the United Forum of IDBI Officers & Employees, said more than 10,000 staff across over 2,000 branches were expected to take part. These participation figures are attributed to the union, not independently confirmed branch-by-branch by the bank.

Employees protest kyun kar rahe hain?

The union’s stated opposition is to the proposed privatisation and transfer of control of IDBI Bank to a Fairfax group entity. The Government’s Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs approved 10955230 Canada Inc., whose parent is Fairfax Financial Holdings Ltd., as the strategic buyer for a 60.72% stake on 14 October 2026. The approved purchase consideration is about ₹52,878.08 crore, according to the Ministry of Finance’s Press Information Bureau (PIB).

The proposed stake comprises 30.48% held by the Government of India and 30.24% held by LIC. The PIB release also says the successful bidder still has to present its credentials to the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) under the fit-and-proper assessment and submit a roadmap for regulatory compliance. So, the Cabinet approval should not be read as proof that the ownership transfer has already been completed.

Important distinction: IDBI Bank’s exchange filing only records receipt of the strike notice. The opposition to the sale is the employees’ forum’s stated position, as reported by Bloomberg and other news outlets.

Kaunsi services chalti rehne ki baat bank ne kahi hai?

In its customer advisory posted on 31 October, IDBI Bank said the following channels would remain operational during the proposed strike:

  • Go Mobile+ app and internet banking.
  • UPI and other digital banking channels.
  • ATMs and Customer Service Points (CSPs).
  • Banking correspondent services and Sameep outlets, where available.

The bank has also advised customers to complete important branch-dependent transactions in advance. It said it would endeavour to keep banking transactions normal, but the actual availability of counter services can vary with employee participation and local arrangements.

Temporary relief at other banks’ ATMs

IDBI Bank’s advisory says customers can withdraw cash from non-IDBI Bank ATMs without charge on 2 and 3 November 2026. This is a temporary measure linked to the strike dates, not a permanent change to the bank’s general ATM fee schedule. If you need cash, check the ATM screen before confirming a transaction and retain the receipt.

Borrowers ke liye iska kya matlab hai?

  • Branch paperwork may take longer: If you need to submit loan documents, update account details or resolve an issue that requires staff assistance, check branch availability before travelling.
  • Digital repayments are not automatically suspended: The bank has said digital channels will remain operational. Check your app or payment confirmation after making a transaction.
  • EMI and card due dates have not been changed by the strike notice: No revised repayment schedule, interest rate or loan term was announced in the documents reviewed for this update. Do not assume a due date has moved.
  • Keep records: Save transaction references, receipts and any service request number if a payment or branch request needs follow-up.

This is an operational advisory, not a change to the terms of IDBI loans or deposits. It also does not apply to customers of other banks.

Abhi kya confirm nahi hai?

At the time of this update, the official exchange filing confirms that the bank received the strike notice, and the bank’s customer advisory lists the alternative channels it expects to keep running. The material reviewed does not provide a verified branch-by-branch status for the morning of 3 November, nor does it confirm that every branch is closed. The union’s participation estimate is not a bank-confirmed headcount.

The final completion date of the proposed stake sale is also not established by the strike notice. The PIB says the buyer’s RBI fit-and-proper assessment and regulatory compliance roadmap remain part of the process. No immediate change to customer account balances, deposit terms, loan rates or EMIs was announced in the sources reviewed.

Aaj IDBI customers kya karein?

If your work can be completed through UPI, Net Banking or Go Mobile+, use the bank’s official digital channels. Agar branch jaana zaroori hai, to nikalne se pehle IDBI Bank se latest branch status confirm kar lein. For cash, the bank’s temporary non-IDBI ATM fee relief applies only to 2–3 November, as stated in its advisory.

Use only the bank’s official website or verified social account for updates. Do not share an OTP, PIN, password or screen-sharing access with anyone claiming to help during the strike.
