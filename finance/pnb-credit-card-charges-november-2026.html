PNB Credit Card Charges From 1 November 2026: New Fees Explained

PNB ka revised credit-card fee schedule 1 November 2026 se effective hai. Yahan cash advance, late payment, reward redemption, utility bills aur failed auto-debit se judi important changes simple language mein samjhein.

LoansBharti Editorial Desk · 2 November 2026 · 5 min read

Punjab National Bank (PNB) ke credit cards par revised service charges 1 November 2026 se lagu ho gaye hain. PNB ke official notice mein cash advance, late payment, cheque/ECS/auto-debit return, duplicate statement, reward redemption aur utility bill payment se related fee changes diye gaye hain.

Sabse important baat: Yeh PNB ka bank-specific fee revision hai, koi naya RBI-wide credit-card rule nahi. Exact charge aapke card variant aur uske applicable terms par depend karega.

1 November se kaun-se charges badle?

PNB ke official revised schedule ke mutabik, applicable Visa/RuPay credit-card variants ke liye key changes yeh hain:

  • Cash advance at PNB ATM: 2.5% of amount withdrawn ya ₹400, jo bhi zyada ho.
  • Cash advance at other domestic ATMs: 2.5% ya ₹500, jo bhi zyada ho.
  • Cash advance at overseas ATMs: 2.5% ya ₹500, jo bhi zyada ho.
  • Late payment fee: Total amount due ₹500 tak — nil; ₹501–₹1,000 — ₹500; ₹1,001–₹10,000 — ₹750; ₹10,000–₹50,000 — ₹1,000; aur ₹50,000 se zyada — ₹1,200. PNB notice mein ₹10,000 boundary dono middle slabs mein repeat hoti hai; agar aapka exact due ₹10,000 hai, bank se applicable slab confirm karein.
  • Cheque return / ECS / auto-debit dishonour: Applicable Visa/RuPay variants par payment amount ka 2%, minimum ₹400. FD-backed cards ke liye notice ₹100 per instrument batata hai.
  • Duplicate statement: Eligible Visa/RuPay variants par kisi bhi month ka duplicate statement ₹50; e-statements free rahenge.
  • Reward redemption: Eligible Visa/RuPay cards par har redemption request ke liye ₹99.
  • Utility bill payments: Agar billing cycle mein total utility payments ₹50,000 se zyada ho jaate hain, toh 1% fee apply hogi, maximum ₹2,000 per transaction.

GST note: PNB ke notice ke mutabik listed fees applicable GST se exclusive hain. Isliye jahan fee lagegi, GST alag se add ho sakta hai.

Late payment slab ko dhyan se samjhein

Late payment fee total amount due ke slab ke hisaab se hai. PNB ke published PDF mein ₹10,000 figure ₹1,001–₹10,000 aur ₹10,000–₹50,000 dono ranges mein aata hai. Is boundary par document overlap karta hai; exact ₹10,000 outstanding ho toh PNB se written clarification lena sensible rahega. Due date miss karne par applicable fee ke alawa card terms ke hisaab se finance charges bhi lag sakte hain.

Yeh changes kin cardholders ko affect karte hain?

Notice mein revised charges Visa/RuPay credit-card variants ke liye diye gaye hain. PNB ke kuch co-branded, corporate, fixed-deposit-backed, metal aur special cards ke liye alag provisions ya exceptions listed hain. Isliye ek card ka charge doosre PNB card par automatically apply mat samjhein. Apne exact card name/variant ke against official schedule check karein.

Cardholders ab kya check karein?

  • PNB app, statement ya card documents se apna exact card variant identify karein.
  • Cash advance lene se pehle applicable fee aur interest terms check karein; credit card cash withdrawal ko normal purchase jaisa treat na karein.
  • Reward points redeem karne se pehle ₹99 redemption fee aur applicable GST ko redemption value ke against compare karein.
  • Auto-debit set hai toh payment date par linked account mein sufficient balance rakhein, taaki return fee ka risk na ho.
  • Paper/duplicate statement ke bajay free e-statement option use karein, agar aapko digital copy acceptable hai.
  • Payment due date aur total amount due ko statement se verify karein. Minimum due pay karna full outstanding clear karne ke barabar nahi hota.

Kya abhi confirm nahi hai?

Official notice listed service charges ko revise karta hai. Yeh notice apne aap mein PNB ke purchase interest/finance-charge rate, credit limit ya EMI conversion terms mein change announce nahi karta. Har co-branded ya special card par same fee lagegi, aisa assume nahi karna chahiye. Individual card variant ka latest MITC aur PNB ka applicable schedule final reference hona chahiye.
