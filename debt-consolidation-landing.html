YOUR QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Debt Consolidation – FAQs

Understand how loan consolidation works before you decide.

Can I merge all my personal loans without providing property as security?

Some banks and NBFCs offer unsecured personal loans that may be used to consolidate eligible existing loans. Whether your loans can be included depends on the lender’s product, your income, credit profile and repayment obligations.

How much can I save after combining personal loans and credit-card dues?

There is no fixed saving amount. Compare the total amount you would pay on your existing loans with the new loan’s total repayment, including interest, processing fees, foreclosure charges and the repayment tenure. A lower EMI can still cost more overall if the tenure is longer.

What documents do I need to apply for loan consolidation?

Lenders commonly ask for PAN, identity and address proof, recent salary slips, salary-account bank statements, and statements or outstanding details for existing loans and credit cards. Exact requirements vary by lender.

Can I combine personal loans and credit-card dues into one EMI?

Some lenders may allow eligible personal-loan balances and credit-card dues to be repaid through one new loan. The lender decides which account types and outstanding amounts can be included.

Will my credit card close automatically after consolidation?

No. Taking a consolidation loan does not automatically close a credit-card account. The card’s status depends on the card issuer’s terms and any separate request or action you make with the issuer.

Will applying for debt consolidation affect my CIBIL score?

A lender’s credit check may appear as an enquiry on your credit report. Your score can also be affected by repayment history, credit utilisation and how accounts are reported. Paying the new EMI on time may support your repayment history over time.

Do all banks and NBFCs have the same eligibility criteria?

No. Each lender sets its own criteria and may review your income, employment, existing EMIs, credit history, repayment record and location before deciding whether to make an offer.

Should I stop paying my current EMIs after submitting an enquiry?

No. Continue paying existing EMIs and credit-card dues until the relevant lender confirms that the selected balances have been paid and the accounts have been updated or closed. Submitting an enquiry does not settle an existing debt.
