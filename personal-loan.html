What to compare before accepting a personal loan

Look beyond the advertised interest rate or monthly EMI. Review the complete cost and repayment terms for the specific offer made to you.

  • Interest rate and whether it can change during the loan term, where applicable.
  • Processing fee and any insurance or other third-party charges.
  • EMI, repayment tenure, total amount payable and the repayment schedule.
  • Late-payment or penal charges and any prepayment or foreclosure conditions.

Ask the lender to explain the written offer and all applicable charges before you accept. Applying to several lenders in a short period can create multiple credit enquiries; see TransUnion CIBIL’s explanation of credit enquiries.
