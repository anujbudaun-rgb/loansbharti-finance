FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions before you apply

Some useful things to know before submitting a loan enquiry.

Does LoansBharti directly provide loans?

LoansBharti is a loan enquiry and facilitation platform. Loan offers, eligibility, interest rates, fees and approval are determined by the relevant lender or provider.

Can multiple loans and credit-card dues be consolidated?

Depending on the product available and your profile, eligible personal-loan balances or credit-card dues may be considered for consolidation. Availability is not guaranteed.

Will my monthly EMI definitely become lower?

Not necessarily. A longer repayment tenure may reduce the monthly instalment but increase the total interest paid. Compare the EMI, fees, rate, tenure and total repayment amount before accepting an offer.

Will submitting an enquiry guarantee loan approval?

No. Submitting an enquiry is not a loan application approval or offer. The relevant lender independently assesses your profile and makes the final decision.

Do I need to pay LoansBharti to submit an enquiry?

Submitting this website enquiry does not require a payment. Never transfer money to anyone claiming they can guarantee loan approval or ask for an advance fee to release a loan.
