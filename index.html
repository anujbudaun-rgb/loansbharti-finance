FAQ

Questions before you apply

What is loan consolidation?

Loan consolidation combines eligible outstanding loans and credit-card dues into one new loan through a bank or NBFC. Instead of managing multiple repayments, you may have one monthly EMI, subject to the lender’s assessment.

Can I combine multiple personal loans and credit-card dues?

Depending on your income, credit profile, repayment history and the lender’s policy, more than one personal loan and eligible credit-card dues may be considered together.

Can loan consolidation reduce my monthly EMI?

It may reduce your monthly EMI if the new loan offers a suitable interest rate or longer repayment tenure. A longer tenure can also increase the total interest paid, so compare the overall repayment amount, processing fee and other charges.

Can I consolidate loans without providing property as security?

Some lenders offer unsecured personal-loan consolidation. Availability and loan terms depend on your profile and the lender’s eligibility criteria.

What documents may be required?

Commonly requested documents include PAN, Aadhaar or other KYC/address proof, recent salary slips and salary-account bank statements. The lender may ask for additional documents after reviewing your profile.

Who decides the interest rate, tenure and loan amount?

The bank or NBFC reviews your income, credit history, existing obligations and repayment record before deciding the interest rate, tenure, eligible amount and final offer.
